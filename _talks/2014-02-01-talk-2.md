At the 22nd International Conference of AI in Medicine (AIME 2024), I was honored to deliver an invited talk on the integration of artificial intelligence in biomedical research. The conference brought together leading experts to discuss advancements in AI applications across various medical domains.

My presentation focused on the utilization of AI methodologies to enhance drug discovery processes, emphasizing the importance of interdisciplinary approaches in bridging computational techniques with clinical applications.

The event featured keynote speeches from renowned professionals in the field and provided a platform for collaborative discussions on the future of AI in medicine.
