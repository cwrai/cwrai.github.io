Participated in ICIBM 2014, an international conference focusing on the integration of computational methods with biology and medicine. The event gathered researchers from bioinformatics, systems biology, and biomedical informatics to discuss data-driven approaches, modeling techniques, and interdisciplinary strategies for understanding complex biological systems and improving healthcare outcomes.
